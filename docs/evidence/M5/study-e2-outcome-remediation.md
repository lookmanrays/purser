## Remediation register and preflight

This is the first remediation of the M5 model-to-E2-outcome integration. The collector recovery and wire-accounting findings concerned different authorities. The original scripted E2 closure fields had already been corrected in M3. The model implementation bypassed that accepted authority with its own outcome algorithm. The current scoring cluster and continuous study implementation session have used zero Resets.

The high-consequence invariant applies to the six frozen E2 fields and their clean, stress, per-seed, incomplete and aggregate consumers across all three arms. Each distinct, observable closed-to-open transition of W1 or W3 counts, including repeats by the same sibling. W2 and the parent each assert a revised retry requirement only through an actual public closure on their exact candidate. The accepted authority grades that assertion on revised W2. An open or rejected closure cannot establish a stale declaration. A lost endpoint may still leave a witnessed reopening, a failing declaration or a lower bound. [Accepted field definitions](../M3/e2-closure-fields.md) · [Frozen protocol](../../../experiments/manifests/v1/protocol.md)

The structural inventory is the E2 public publication stream, the registered outcome fields in `experiments.e2.outcomes`, its grade keys and its comparison and reporting callers. The model reader collapsed repeated reopenings to unique names, inverted the stale-at-delivery predicate, and graded parent retry declarations on every final criterion. It also sampled continuing closures at later reads, conflated terminal coverage with observed lower bounds, and exposed neither the first stale versus new declaration nor the per-publication evidence in its report. These errors share one cause. The model integration redefined the scripted measurement.

The replacement will deliver validated model action chronology, physical plan versions or kernel transactions, original E2 notice and original isolated candidate grades to the existing E2 outcome scorer. Reuse its publication ordering, duplicate suppression, exact-versus-observed fields, layer for the changed retry requirement and all-obligation final score. Preserve the existing model receipt, cost, frozen source and qualification boundaries. No provider or topology change is needed. Read-only regressions will cover repeated transitions, an open or refused closure, closed old retry, corrected retry with an unrelated failure, and partial observation. Qualification will use a read-only replay of archived model cases and fresh controls after release of the independent observer's serial guest window. The prospective real-model study remains unregistered.
