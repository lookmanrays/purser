# P4-10 Contradiction and local re-plan

## Goal

A worker that finds a contract unworkable files a contradiction; the contract's owner re-plans, and only the dependent region goes stale.

## Context

Design: The engineer loop (return paths) and Escalation rules.

## Design

- Kernel operator `contradiction.raise` (v2): executor role, fields target, reason, evidence digests; creates a `Contradiction` record and pauses the raising node.
- Routing: a function contract contradiction goes to its module lead; a module or boundary contract goes to the architect; a decision goes to the principal.
- Resolution: a new contract version or decision; `contradiction.resolve` links it, and `impact` marks only dependents of the changed contract stale.
- Escalation counts per contract feed the redesign flag.

## Out of scope

Router learning (P5-07).

## Test plan

Fixture plan with two modules; a contradiction on one function contract; resolution; the untouched module keeps running.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1 Raising a contradiction pauses the node and records the target and reason.
- AC-2 It is routed to the owner by contract kind.
- AC-3 After resolution only dependents of the changed contract are stale; others keep their state.
- AC-4 A contract with three contradictions is flagged for redesign.

## Definition of done

Standard Definition of done.

## Escalate if

A contradiction's target has no recorded owner.
