# P3-09 Planning trial

## Goal

Five feature tasks from your own projects planned end to end by Purser, each reviewed by you.

## Context

P3 gate: five valid plans you approve without rewriting.

## Design

- Pick five tasks from the feature-bench candidates, at levels 2 and 3.
- Run `purser plan` live (not replay); record bundles, validator reports, token use and wall-clock under `bench/plan/<task>/`.
- You review each plan and record approve, approve with edits (with the edits listed) or reject.
- Recorded model exchanges become new golden fixtures for P3-03 and P3-04.

## Out of scope

Execution of the plans.

## Test plan

None; the outcome is your review.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1 Five plans pass the validator with no findings.
- AC-2 You approve at least five plans without rewriting them; edits, if any, are listed.
- AC-3 Token use and cost per plan are recorded.

## Definition of done

Results and your verdicts committed under `bench/plan/`.

## Escalate if

Plans need edits on most tasks; that is a design issue, not a prompt tweak.
