# P1-07 Decisions and dispatch

## Goal

The decision-support operators from purser-main: assumptions, decisions, opinions, work dependencies, premise observations with invalidation, and assignment dispatch.

## Context

Spec: `decisions-v1.md`.

## Design

- Operators: `assumption.create`, `decision.record`, `decision.opine`, `dependency.add`, `dependency.remove`, `assumption.observe` and `assignment.dispatch`, as specified.
- `decision.record` with `supersedes` keeps the predecessor's history and redirects its incoming work dependencies to the new decision in the same event.
- `assumption.observe` stores the premise artifact. On a first contradiction it invalidates the assumption and applies `impact.Affected` in the same batch, at the observation's revision.
- Changing a dependency of assigned work fences its generation.
- The package registers reducers for every event in the reference `decisionevents.py`.
- `assignment.dispatch` is the local release cutoff. Every physical callback attempt gets a distinct proposal ID.

## Out of scope

Approvals (in the gate since P1-03) and effects (P1-08a).

## Test plan

The decision example in `decisions-v1.md`, the continuity overlay, a supersede with redirected dependencies, a contradiction outside triggers, a dependency cycle and repeated dispatch attempts.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1 The `decisions-v1.md` example produces a `PremiseObserved` event with the specified fields.
- AC-2 A first contradiction of a trigger assumption marks the decision reconsidering and its dependent work stale, in one batch.
- AC-3 Superseding a reconsidering decision redirects its incoming dependencies and keeps its history queryable.
- AC-4 Confirmation, or a contradiction outside the triggers, leaves the decision effective.
- AC-5 A dependency cycle exposes its unmet prerequisites through `why`-level reasons.
- AC-6 Dispatch is admitted only while the assignment's support and generation qualify.
- AC-7 Folding each golden log from `tests/test_decisions.py` through the registered reducers reproduces every recorded snapshot.

## Definition of done

Standard Definition of done.

## Escalate if

Invalidation cannot be applied in the observation's own batch.
