# P6-03 Design approval through tam

## Goal

The design is attached to the tam task, and approval arrives either as a status change out of design review or as a comment keyword; the first answer wins and a conflicting later answer is flagged.

## Context

Design: Tracker integration (Approval); you chose to support both forms.

## Design

- On a held plan at level 2 or 3, attach the design (decisions, contracts, graph summary, validator report) and move the task to design review.
- Watch for a status change or a comment from an authorized human containing `approve` or `reject` with a reason.
- The first valid answer is recorded as the principal's `approval.answer`; any later conflicting answer produces a comment and a flag, not a second decision.
- Only comments from configured principal accounts count.

## Out of scope

Slice approval at level 3 beyond the first design.

## Test plan

Status-first, comment-first, both in conflict, a comment from an unauthorized account.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1 A status change out of design review approves the held plan.
- AC-2 A comment `approve` from a principal approves; `reject <reason>` denies with the reason.
- AC-3 After the first answer, a conflicting answer is flagged and does not change the decision.
- AC-4 A keyword from an unauthorized account is ignored and logged.

## Definition of done

Standard Definition of done.

## Escalate if

tam cannot tell Purser who changed a status.
