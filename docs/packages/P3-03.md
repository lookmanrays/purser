# P3-03 Architect session

## Goal

The architect role: from a task, its acceptance criteria and a system-graph slice, produce a plan bundle (decisions, boundary contracts, module work graph, acceptance tests) and propose it through the gate, held for principal approval at levels 2 and 3.

## Context

Design: The engineer loop (Learn, Design, Plan) and Data contracts. Split before ready: likely P3-03a plan bundle format and kernel operators, P3-03b architect prompt pack and session. P3-05 then depends on P3-03a only, so the validator and the architect session can be built in parallel.

## Design

- Plan bundle (canonical JSON): `decisions[]` (choice, alternatives, rationale, assumptions, triggers), `contracts[]` (contract file contents), `work[]` (ID, parent, spec), `tests[]` (obligation drafts with criterion text and acceptance mapping), `dependencies[]`, `estimate`.
- Kernel operator `contract.register` (v2): stores the contract file as an artifact and creates a `Contract` record with its digest, producer and consumers.
- `Propose(bundle)` converts the bundle into one proposal: `decision.record`, `contract.register`, `work.create`, `work.spec`, `obligation.set` and `dependency.add`, held with `needs_principal` at levels 2 and 3.
- Session: prompt pack under `prompts/architect/` (system prefix, output schema, two worked examples); the model sees the context pack and returns the bundle as structured output; at most two repair rounds using validator failures (P3-05) before escalating to the human.
- Every model call runs in replay mode in CI.

## Out of scope

Module-level design (P3-04) and the validator itself (P3-05).

## Test plan

Golden tasks with recorded model responses; bundles that convert to the expected proposals; a bundle with an invalid contract; approval and rejection paths.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC-1 Each golden task's recorded response yields a bundle equal to its golden file.
- AC-2 `Propose` turns a golden bundle into the exact ops in its golden proposal.
- AC-3 At level 2 the proposal is held with `needs_principal`; approval admits all ops in one batch.
- AC-4 `contract.register` records the digest of the exact file bytes, and a second registration of different bytes under the same ID and version is rejected.
- AC-5 After two failed repair rounds the session stops and reports the validator failures.

## Definition of done

Standard Definition of done. Split into packages of at most 600 changed lines before status ready.

## Escalate if

The bundle cannot express a design decision the golden tasks need.
