## Invariant and circuit breaker

**Cluster.** E2 comparison eligibility after evidence reconstruction. Its historical first Reset is recorded in [the comparison Reset](comparison-reset.md). The reconciled Preflight explicitly included a never-released primary and measurement of a named candidate even at START in its candidate-versus-exit checks. The same separation is required by [the fresh design](e2-recovery-design.md). Stopped-worker recovery and the case reader jointly supply the independently known candidate observations used in those comparisons. The public-work admission producer has a separate invariant.

**Earlier findings.** A clean label and apparently healthy endpoint produced a numerical comparison after an actual change had been delivered. A stopped worker's Git observations also disappeared when a disposable bare repository was unavailable despite surviving sealed packs. The replacement joined the original-history reader and protected measurement changes, restored packs independently and derived eligibility per measure.

**Why the replacement missed this case.** The durable snapshot can already contain an authenticated starting pack while its participant cost basis is `before_release`. The early return in `experiments/e2/trial.py:147` skips the sealed-candidate measurement pass for this entire phase. The live attempt also conditions retention on participant release. Restart processing, independent Git observation and the consumer's strict remeasurement thus need separate evidence about candidate availability and participant lifecycle. The reader can restore the pack and still rerun zero criteria because the archive declares none. A check with no initial pack and a check after parent closure covered the two outer cases while leaving this physically different middle case untested.

**Scope of failure.** The recovered raw record discards eight measurable versioned observations. This invalidates general claims for stopped-worker measurement, strict completeness and source readiness. It preserves the observed zero cost, missing participant exit, individual positive results below and prior legacy results at their own sources. The first Reset for the comparison cluster was already consumed. The fresh primary used one Reset for public-work measurement. This finding consumes no further Reset. The review-remediation post-replacement rule closes implementation in this context.
